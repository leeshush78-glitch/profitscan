💡 UK buying & selling checklist

Before buying, check recent sold listings, exact model, condition, delivery charges, platform fees, packaging, potential returns and demand. Don't rely on asking prices.

Before selling, photograph the actual item, describe all faults, confirm payment in your account, use tracked postage where appropriate and keep proof of posting. Avoid off-platform payment requests and suspicious courier links.

Regular buying specifically to resell for profit can count as trading. Keep records of sales, costs and receipts; check HMRC rules and any applicable tax obligations. Consumer rights and platform rules may differ for business sellers.
